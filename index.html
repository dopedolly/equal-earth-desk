Why this projection

Web Mercator, the projection behind nearly every map on the internet, inflates land the further it sits from the equator — rendering Greenland the apparent size of Africa when Africa is fourteen times larger. A publication about the whole world should not open by misreporting the size of most of it. The Equal Earth projection was designed in 2018 by Bojan Šavrič, Tom Patterson and Bernhard Jenny, and is free for anyone to use.

Why no photographs

Every card carries the outline of the country it comes from, drawn from the same geometry as the map above. No stock imagery, no borrowed photos, nothing that needs licensing — the shape of the place is the picture.

How stories are chosen

Consequence is uncapped: large economies generate more consequential news and we do not suppress that. But three slots in every ten go to stories that are unusually big for where they happened, measured against that country's own baseline. A major story out of Barbados carries more signal than Britain's fifth routine one.
